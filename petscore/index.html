Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What PetScore is

PetScore is a website where people post photos of their pets and rate each other's posts from 1 to 10.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text), or, if you use GitHub sign-in, the email and account identifier GitHub shares with us.
  • Profile: the username you choose and the date you joined. Other signed-in users can find you by username and open a profile page showing your posts they are allowed to see, with your average score.
  • Content you post: pet photos, pet names, captions, ratings and comments, and who you chose to share a photo with.
  • Safety records: reports you send about a post, and moderation actions taken on posts, comments or accounts.
  • Your agreement: the date you accepted this policy.

We do not run advertising or sell your data, and we do not use tracking cookies. Your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay signed in.

Who can see what

  • Photos set to Everyone are visible to all signed-in users. Specific people photos are visible only to you and the people you picked. Nobody photos are visible only to you.
  • Your email address is not shown to other users.
  • Moderators can see reported and hidden posts and the comments on them. Admins can also see usernames, moderator and ban status, and an activity log. Staff access is limited by the site's database security rules.

How we use it

To run your account, show posts and ratings, keep the community safe (review reports, hide content, restrict accounts that break the rules), and keep the service working.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Photos are kept in a private storage area and shown through short-lived links. GitHub sign-in is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any of your own posts at any time, which also removes its ratings and comments. You can delete your account and all its data yourself in Settings, or contact us at the address below. Safety records may be kept for a reasonable time to prevent repeat abuse.

Children

PetScore is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
